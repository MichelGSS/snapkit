# Draw attention with highlights, spotlight and magnifier

The tools in the **Highlight** group help readers find the important part of a busy screenshot.

## Highlighter

Marks text the way a highlighter pen does, with a translucent yellow band that keeps the text readable.

1. Select **Highlighter**.
2. Drag over the text.

To use another color, select the highlight with **Select** and pick a color in the options bar.

## Spotlight

Darkens everything except one area, so the eye goes straight to it.

1. Select **Spotlight**.
2. Drag over the area that should stay bright.

Use one spotlight per image. To point at several things, combine one spotlight with arrows or [numbered markers](number-steps.md).

## Magnifier

Shows an enlarged copy of a small detail, such as a tiny icon or a field value, next to the original.

1. Select **Magnifier**.
2. Drag over the detail you want to enlarge.

   SnapKit places the enlarged copy next to it, inside the image, and selects the copy. The original area stays visible, with an outline.

3. Drag the copy to where you want it, and drag its corners to resize it. The aspect ratio is kept.
4. Use the **Zoom** slider to change how much the copy is enlarged.

You can also drag the small handles on the original area to change which part is enlarged.
