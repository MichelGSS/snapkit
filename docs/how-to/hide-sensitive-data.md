# Hide passwords and personal data

Screenshots often show more than you meant to share: email addresses, customer names, tokens, internal URLs. Hide them before you copy or save the image.

1. In the editor or the quick annotation toolbar, select **Pixelate** or **Blur**.
2. Drag over each piece of information you want to hide.

| Tool | Result | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Pixelate** | Large, clearly visible blocks | Making it obvious that something was removed |
| **Blur** | A soft blur, strong enough to make screen text unreadable | A cleaner look in polished documentation |

Both tools change the pixels of the final image. Once you copy or save it, the hidden text can't be recovered from the file.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Check the whole image before you share it, including thumbnails, tab titles, notification badges and the taskbar. Use [Spotlight](highlight-and-magnify.md#spotlight) or [Crop](crop-resize-frame.md) to remove whole areas you don't need.

## Remove a strip with private content

If a whole row of a table or a block of a page shouldn't appear at all, use **Cut out** in the **Image** group: drag down over the rows to remove a horizontal strip, or sideways to remove a vertical one. The two remaining parts are joined.
