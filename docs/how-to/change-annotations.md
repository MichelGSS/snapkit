# Change or remove an annotation

In SnapKit, every arrow, shape, text and marker stays a separate object until you copy or save the image. You can select it again and change it at any time while the capture is open.

## Select an annotation

1. Select **Select** in the tool column (or in the quick annotation toolbar).
2. Select the annotation on the image.

The annotation shows its outline and small white handles. The options bar shows **Selected:** and the annotation's type, with its options.

Rectangles and ellipses are selected by their outline, so you can still select what's inside them. Filled shapes are selected anywhere.

## Move, resize or restyle it

- **Move:** drag the annotation, or press the arrow keys to move it 1 pixel at a time (10 pixels with **Shift**).
- **Resize:** drag one of the white handles. For an arrow or a line, the handles are the two ends.
- **Restyle:** change the color, width or any other option in the options bar. For a step marker, you can also change its number in **No.**
- **Edit text:** double-click a text or callout, change the text, and press **Enter**.

## Duplicate or delete it

- **Duplicate:** press **Ctrl+D**, or select **Duplicate** in the options bar.
- **Delete:** press **Delete** or **Backspace**, or select **Delete** in the options bar.

## Erase part of the image

The **Eraser** works on the pixels, not on whole objects: wherever you drag it, the image goes back to the original capture. Use it to clean up part of a pen stroke or a highlight.

## Undo and redo

- **Undo:** **Ctrl+Z**, or **Undo** in the options bar.
- **Redo:** **Ctrl+Y** or **Ctrl+Shift+Z**, or **Redo** in the options bar.

A whole drag counts as one step, so one **Ctrl+Z** undoes a full move.

## What you can't change later

- Annotations made before a **Crop**, **Cut out**, rotation or resize become part of the image. Do those changes first and annotate afterward.
- When you reopen a capture from the library, earlier annotations are already part of the image. See [How annotations work](../explanation/annotations.md).
