# Capture menus and tooltips with a delay

Menus, drop-down lists and tooltips close as soon as you press a key or click somewhere else. A delay gives you time to open them before SnapKit takes the shot.

## Set a delay

1. Select **Options** in the SnapKit top bar.
2. Under **Wait before capturing**, select **Delay 3 s**, **Delay 5 s** or **Delay 10 s**.

The **Options** button shows the delay while it's on. It applies to every capture until you select **No delay**.

## Capture a menu

1. Set a delay of 5 seconds.
2. Press **Alt+Shift+S** and select the area where the menu will open.
3. Press **Enter** to confirm the area.

   SnapKit minimizes its window and the status bar shows **Capturing in 5 s…**.

4. Open the menu in the other app and leave it open.

When the time is up, SnapKit takes the shot and opens it in the editor.

> [!NOTE]
> With a delay, the region capture doesn't use quick annotation, because the screen changes after you select the area. The capture always opens in the editor.
