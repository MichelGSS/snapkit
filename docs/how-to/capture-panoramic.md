# Capture an area you scroll yourself (panoramic)

Panoramic capture builds one image while you scroll an area by hand, in any direction. Use it for maps, diagrams, design canvases, spreadsheets, and pages where automatic scrolling doesn't work well.

1. Select **Panoramic** in the SnapKit top bar.
2. Drag over the area you're going to scroll, or select a window. Keep the area a little smaller than the content's visible part.
3. If you dragged an area, press **Enter**, or select **Capture**, to confirm it.

   A small panel appears next to the area.

4. Select **Start**.
5. Scroll the area slowly with the mouse wheel, the scroll bars or by dragging the content, in any direction. The panel shows the current size of the image.
6. Select **Stop** when you've covered everything.

The result opens in the editor.

## Tips

- **Scroll slowly.** Each new shot has to overlap the previous one. If you go too fast, the panel shows **Too fast** and how many shots didn't fit. Scroll back a little and continue more slowly; SnapKit continues from the last good shot.
- **You can go back.** Scrolling back over parts you already captured doesn't hurt the result.
- **Fixed parts are fine.** A fixed sidebar or a patterned background behind the content doesn't stop the stitching.

To cancel, select **Cancel** in the panel.
